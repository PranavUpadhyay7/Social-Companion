# SceneMates Design System

## Product

SceneMates helps people discover nightlife events, meet clubbers, and form groups. The experience should feel cinematic, selective, youthful, and premium without becoming visually noisy.

## Visual foundation

- Use only black, near-black `#101010`, white, zinc gray, muted gray text, and violet `#a95bf4`.
- Violet is an accent and glow color, never a large filled surface.
- Use Geist sans throughout. Do not introduce serif, script, or decorative fonts.
- Headlines are uppercase, bold or medium weight, tightly composed, and widely tracked where appropriate.
- Body copy is concise, clean, gray, and easy to scan.
- Prefer compact 4–6px radii, fine zinc borders, and flat dark surfaces.
- Primary actions are white with dark text and may gain a restrained violet glow on hover.
- Secondary controls remain black with zinc borders.

## Layout and spacing

- Favor generous negative space, strong alignment, and a clear single focal point.
- Desktop content may use a maximum width near 1540px with responsive horizontal padding.
- Avoid crowded dashboards, excessive chips, repeated decorative labels, and dense metadata.
- Event browsing should lead with one editorial featured event followed by a calm category row and a spacious card grid.

## Background and motion

- Use a pure black canvas.
- White and `#a95bf4` volumetric rays may flow subtly from the top-right, matching the landing page.
- Motion should be ambient and restrained; hover movement should be short and subtle.

## Components

- Navigation: translucent black, thin zinc lower border, italic bold white SceneMates wordmark.
- Cards: black/near-black, hairline zinc border, compact radius, strong photography, minimal metadata.
- Inputs: black translucent surface, zinc border, muted placeholder.
- Buttons: uppercase, small, bold, wide tracking; white primary or black secondary.

## Hard constraints

- No colorful gradients, purple-filled panels, glassmorphism overload, neon rainbow effects, giant pills, or generic SaaS-dashboard styling.
- Preserve accessibility, legibility, and production-plausible responsive structure.
